About Starlight

Welcome to Starlight: Star Logic Puzzle, an elegant and satisfying spatial logic-puzzle game designed for puzzle enthusiasts and casual gamers seeking a serene, dark-mode challenge. Maneuver cosmic glass lenses across deep space grid maps, transporting glowing Star Orbs into unlit constellation sockets. Think ahead, plan your moves, and ignite every constellation!

✨ How to Play:

1
Slide Cosmic Lenses: Swipe to maneuver frosted glass lenses carrying Star Orbs across the deep space grid.
2
Align with Sky Sockets: Position Star Orbs directly over corresponding unlit constellation sockets.
3
Ignite Constellations: Locking Star Orbs in place illuminates the sky with brilliant celestial flares.
4
Use Empty Lenses: Once emptied, glass lenses remain on the board to help position remaining lenses and navigate around monoliths!
