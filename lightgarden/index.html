About Light Garden: Shade Puzzle

Light Garden: Shade Puzzle invites you into a tranquil, sunlit courtyard sanctuary. Built on the elegant foundations of classical Japanese grid logic (Kuromasu / Kurodoko), your objective is to position charred-cedar privacy trellis shades across garden beds so that every flower receives its exact required rays of sunlight.

🌿 Spatial Deduction & Sunbeam Routing

☀️ Sunlight Requirements: Each flower tile displays a number indicating how many unshaded stepping-stone tiles it must see along horizontal and vertical lines of sight.
🪵 Charred-Cedar Trellis Shades: Place dark wooden trellis screens to block excess light and focus illumination directly where needed.
🌸 Connected Stepping Stones: All sunlit garden tiles must remain contiguously connected as a single garden path — no isolated pockets allowed!
🪨 Non-Adjacent Shades: Trellis shades cannot touch each other orthogonally, providing an intuitive, deeply satisfying deductive constraint.

60 Handcrafted Levels Across 3 Garden Suites

Journey through three distinct botanical environments, each presenting escalating logical depth:

1. The Courtyard Terrace

Gentle 5x5 and 6x6 introductory garden grids to master sunbeam lines and trellis shade placement.

2. The Shaded Pergola

Expanding layouts featuring complex overlapping light rays, corner intersections, and dual-flower constraints.

3. The Glass Conservatory

Master-level spatial puzzles demanding rigorous deductive chains and holistic garden path topology.
