Sumplete: Sum Delete

ChatGPT Inspired Math Logic
Math & Logic

A clever fusion of Sudoku and Nonogram puzzle rules! Inspired by ChatGPT, your goal is to mark numbers on the grid to be deleted so that the remaining numbers add up to the correct row and column target sums.

🔴 Red: Mark numbers red to exclude them from the sum.
🟢 Green: Mark numbers green to keep them in the sum.
✨ Target indicators turn green once you solve the exact row/column sum!

Filler: Flood It

Color Conquest Strategy
Color Flood

A fast-paced color flood puzzle game. Starting from your territory, choose a color each round to flood adjacent regions of the same color, expanding your control until the entire board is flooded!

🎨 Conquest: Pick colors strategically to expand adjacent flooded tiles.
⚔️ Modes: Enjoy a relaxing solo puzzle session or challenge a computer opponent.
🌐 Multiplayer: Compete live vs online players to flood the board first!

Hide The Bones

Sliding Dog Puzzle
Sliding Puzzle

Slide pieces around the board so that all bones drop into the holes! Easy challenges feature 2 to 3 dogs, while advanced brainteasers require managing up to 4 dogs simultaneously.

🦴 Bone Drops: Don't drop bones too early—some holes require specific dogs!
🐕 Passage: Each hole fits 1 bone. Once filled, other dogs can safely slide over.
🧩 Dozens of progressively tricky levels to test your spatial reasoning.

Nonogram

Pixel Art Picture Logic
Picture Logic

Classic Japanese picture logic puzzles! Use discrete tomography clues listed along the grid margins to fill in or leave cells blank to reveal stunning pixel art artwork.

🔢 Margin Clues: Numbers like "4 8 3" indicate unbroken lines of filled squares.
🖼️ Pixel Art: Deduce column & row patterns to reveal hidden pictures!
🧠 Multiple grid sizes ranging from casual 5x5s to master-level challenges.
