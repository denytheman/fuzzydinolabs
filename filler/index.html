How To Play & Game Rules

The game consists of a board with random colored cells. Players usually start from a white cell at the top-left corner of the board, but starting positions can be customized in different game modes.

Game Objective:

🎨
At each round, choose a new color for your flooded region to absorb adjacent matching cells, expanding your territory across the board.
🏆
Final Goal: Flood the entire board in the fewest moves possible!
🤖
Try a relaxing solo game of Filler, or compete against a computer AI / 1v1 online opponent in real-time strategy matches!
