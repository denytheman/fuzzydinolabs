About Filler: Flood It

Filler is a vibrant, tactical color-flood strategy game. Starting from your base corner, choose a new color each turn to absorb adjacent matching tiles into your territory. Plan moves ahead to claim the majority of the board before your opponent, or solve solo puzzles within move limits!

🎨 How To Play:

1
Choose a Color: Each round, select one of the available colors from the palette.
2
Flood Territory: Your flooded region expands by connecting to all neighboring cells of that color.
3
Conquer the Board: Capture more than half the board in versus mode or clear the entire grid in solo challenges!
