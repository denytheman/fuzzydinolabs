About SpinLogic

SpinLogic is a fast-paced, tactile action puzzle game that reinvents the rotary puzzle genre for modern touchscreens. Guide falling chromatic marbles across dynamic networks of rotating dials, transfer conduits, and color filters!

⚙️ Innovative Rotary Puzzle Action

🔄 Tap to Spin: Turn 4-slot mechanical dials 90° counter-clockwise to organize falling marble colors.
🚀 Conduit Ejection: Tap marbles facing pipe channels to launch them across the network into adjacent dials.
💥 4-of-a-Kind Detonation: Fill all 4 slots of any dial with matching colors to trigger explosive clears, free up space, and build massive combo multipliers!

🌌 5 Visually Stunning Shader Zones

Travel through 5 unique atmospheric realms powered by live 60/120 FPS dynamic background shaders:

1. The Foundry

2-color introductory training amidst molten steel and ambient heat.

2. Cyberpunk District

3 colors and Prismatic Wildcard marbles under neon rain reflections.

3. Clockwork Brass

4 colors, Caged Dials, and Obsidian Bomb explosions amongst spinning gears.

4. Cryo Sub-Zero

5 colors and Cryo Crystal frost-locking dials in sub-zero snowstorms.

5. Quantum Void

All 6 colors and Liquid Painter converters at the cosmic singularity.

🎮 3 Gameplay Modes

Campaign: 100 levels with star ratings & goals.
Time Attack: +10s on color match high-score rush.
Relax Mode: Zen, timer-free pure spatial logic.

✨ Special Power Marbles

🌈 Prismatic Wildcards: Adapts to any hue.
💣 Obsidian Bombs: Vaporizes full dials.
❄️ Cryo Crystals: Shatter frost with rapid taps.
🎨 Liquid Painters: Repaints adjacent marbles.
