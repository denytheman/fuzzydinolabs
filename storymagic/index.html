About Phonics Magic: Learn to Read

Phonics Magic transforms learning to read into an enchanting, confidence-building journey. Using always-on voice recognition technology, the app listens as children read aloud, highlighting words in real-time, providing immediate positive reinforcement, and gently guiding them through tricky phonetic sounds.

🪄 How Phonics Magic Builds Fluent Readers

🎙️ Real-Time Voice Recognition: Children read aloud into their device, watching words sparkle as they correctly pronounce each syllable and sentence.
📚 Decodable Story Library: Rich, illustrated stories organized by phonetic progression (CVC words, blends, digraphs, long vowels).
⚡ Timed Speed Challenges: Fun "30-Second Rush" and "60-Second Marathon" modes build rapid word recognition and sight word automaticity.
🤖 Story Lab AI: Parents and teachers can generate custom decodable stories tailored to a child's favorite themes, names, and current phonetic focus.

Comprehensive Early Literacy Modules

Everything young learners need from preschool through 2nd grade reading mastery:

1. Sight Word Academy

Over 300+ Dolch and Fry high-frequency sight words with multi-sensory audio, phoneme breakdown, and flashcard modes.

2. Interactive Decodables

Charming illustrated storybooks with tap-to-listen word pronunciation and karaoke-style real-time voice tracking.

3. Parent & Teacher Dashboard

Full Word Manager to tag tricky vocabulary, track accuracy statistics, and manage custom reading assignments.
