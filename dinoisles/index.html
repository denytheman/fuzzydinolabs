About Dino Isles

Welcome to Dino Isles: Cozy Logic Puzzle, a soothing and tactile spatial logic puzzle game where you terraform ancient archipelagos for adorable prehistoric companions! Progress through 10 unique biomes from gentle 4x4 Coral Shallows to master 10x10 Pangaea Prime archipelagos.

🏝️ How to Play (The Ancient Laws)

🖐️ Drag to Paint: Touch any dinosaur clue and drag across open water to carve its lush island habitat.
🔢 Match the Target Number: Every dinosaur has a number indicating the exact size of its island (e.g. a Triceratops labeled "4" needs exactly 4 connected tiles).
🚫 The No-Touch Rule: Two dinosaurs of the same size/color can NEVER touch orthogonally—keep them separated with other species!
🌊 Full Grid Coverage: Fill every open water tile on the grid while routing around volcanic obstacles.
↩️ Tap or Backtrack to Erase: Made a mistake? Simply click any colored tile or drag backwards to erase and re-carve your path.
