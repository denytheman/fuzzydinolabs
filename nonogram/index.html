About Nonogram

Nonograms (also known as Hanjie, Picross, or Griddlers) are picture logic puzzles where cells in a grid must be colored or left blank according to side number clues to reveal a hidden pixel art image.

🧩 Margin Clue Rules:

1
Read Row & Column Clues: The numbers measure how many unbroken lines of filled-in squares exist in any given line.
2
Understand Sequences: A clue of "4 8 3" means there are runs of 4, 8, and 3 filled squares in order, separated by at least one blank space.
3
Mark Confirmed & Cross Blanks: Fill confirmed squares and place cross marks on definite empty cells to narrow down the solution.
4
Reveal Pixel Art: Complete every row and column to bring the hidden pixel artwork to life!
