How Nonograms Work

Nonograms are picture logic puzzles in which cells in a grid must be colored or left blank according to numbers at the side of the grid to reveal a hidden pixel art image.

Margin Clue Rules:

🔢
The numbers are a form of discrete tomography measuring how many unbroken lines of filled-in squares exist in any given row or column.
💡
Clue Example: A clue of "4 8 3" means there are sets of four, eight, and three filled squares in that order, separated by at least one blank square between successive sets!
🖼️
Use logical deduction to complete every line and reveal the hidden pixel art artwork.
