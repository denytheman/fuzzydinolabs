About CipherSage

CipherSage combines classic acrostic-style word clues with cryptogram quotes from history’s greatest thinkers. Solve word clues to reveal matching cipher numbers across the quote, then use logic to crack the code and unveil the full message!

🌟 Key Features

🧩 120+ Handcrafted Levels

Progress from simple tutorial ciphers to expert-level historical quotes. Test your vocabulary, logic, and deduction skills!

📅 365 Daily Cryptograms

A brand new puzzle for every single day of the year. Build your daily streak and earn daily rewards!

🏆 28 Trophy Achievements

Collect 28 unique achievements across Progress, Mastery, Daily, and Economy categories with Game Center leaderboards.

💡 Helpful Hint Assists

Reveal Random Letters, illuminate all Vowels (A, E, I, O, U), or spot and correct wrong entries instantly with Check Mistakes.

🎁 Daily Rewards & Milestones

7-Day Daily Login Calendar with escalating rewards plus Bonus Present Gifts (+100 Coins!) for every 7 levels completed.

🎨 Premium Adaptive Design

Beautiful Dark Mode and crisp High-Contrast Light Mode with custom polyphonic background music & sound effects engine.

📴 100% Offline Playable

No internet connection required — play anytime on your commute, travel, or break!
