Game Objective & Tactics

The object of Hide The Bones is to slide the dog pieces around the game board so that all bones fall into the holes on the board.

Puzzle Rules & Strategy:

🐕
Easy challenges have only 2 or 3 dogs while more advanced ones feature 4 dogs to manage!
⚠️
Specific Matches: Some holes can only be filled by a specific dog, so don't drop the bones too early!
🦴
Every hole can contain only 1 bone. Once a hole is filled, other dogs can safely slide over it to reach their destinations.
