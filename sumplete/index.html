About Sumplete

In Sumplete: Sum Delete, your objective is to mark numbers on the grid that need to be eliminated so that the remaining numbers add up to the target sum outside each row and column. With varying grid sizes from 3x3 to 9x9, it's an addictive workout for your brain!

🔢 How to Play:

1
Examine Target Sums: Look at the target numbers positioned at the right and bottom of each row and column.
2
Mark to Delete (Red): Tap a number to mark it red, removing its value from that row and column sum.
3
Confirm Correct (Green): Keep essential numbers green to satisfy the row and column equations.
4
Target Indicator Feedback: Outer indicators illuminate green as soon as the target sum for that line is perfectly satisfied!
