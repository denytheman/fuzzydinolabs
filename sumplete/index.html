How To Play & Rules

In Sumplete: Sum Delete, your objective is to mark numbers on the grid that need to be removed in order for the row and column sums to match the target numbers displayed on the sides.

Game Controls & Indicators:

🔴
Red Mark: Marking a number red excludes it from the current row and column sum calculation.
🟢
Green Mark: Marking a number green keeps it in the row and column sum calculation.
✨
Target Indicators: Once the numbers outside the grid turn green, you know you have achieved the correct sum for that row or column!
