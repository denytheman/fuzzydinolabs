Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 2026

Fuzzy Dino Labs ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting it through our compliance with this Privacy Policy. This policy applies to our mobile applications (including Filler: Color Flood) and website at fuzzydinolabs.com.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information to provide better services to all users of our games and applications:

  • Game & Multiplayer Data: Player nicknames, scores, matchmaking statuses, game moves, and win/loss records required to manage real-time online multiplayer gameplay and leaderboards.
  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version, and unique device identifiers to support analytics and crash reporting.
  • Advertising Identifier: Google Advertising ID (GAID) or Apple IDFA used by Google AdMob to deliver relevant advertisements and verify ad impressions.

2. How We Use Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To operate, maintain, and provide core features of our mobile games.
  • To enable online multiplayer matchmaking, leaderboards, and rating calculations.
  • To serve advertisements via Google AdMob in accordance with your privacy choices.
  • To diagnose crashes, fix bugs, and optimize app performance using Firebase Crashlytics.

3. Third-Party Services & Ad Networks

Our applications integrate trusted third-party services that may process data according to their respective privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob: Serves advertisements and validates publisher inventory via `app-ads.txt`.
  • Firebase (Google LLC): Used for database storage, Crashlytics, and authentication.
  • RevenueCat: Manages in-app purchases and subscription verifications.

4. Children's Privacy

Our applications do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us immediately so we can remove it.
