About DinoDig: Fossil Sweeper

DinoDig: Fossil Sweeper is a pure deductive logic puzzle game set in the world of paleontology. Combining the perimeter line constraints of Nonograms with the localized proximity numbers of Minesweeper, DinoDig delivers a completely deterministic puzzle experience where every single quarry is solvable through logic alone. No 50/50 guesses, no trial-and-error, and no luck required.

The Dual-Constraint Logic System

Perimeter Line Scanners: Edge totals indicate the exact count of fossilized bone segments buried in each row and column.
Subsurface Acoustic Sensors: Chiseled rock tiles reveal proximity numbers indicating how many surrounding 3x3 tiles contain delicate fossil remains.
Cross-Referencing Deduction: When local neighbor tiles seem ambiguous, perimeter totals provide the missing constraint to guarantee safe progress.
Tactile Field Tools: Strike barren limestone with the Steel Chisel, or apply protective Plaster Jackets over delicate fossil bones. Protected tiles cannot be accidentally chiseled.
Guaranteed Safe Starts & Cascades: Every quarry provides at least one zero-fossil line to begin excavating with confidence, triggering natural empty-pocket cascades.
